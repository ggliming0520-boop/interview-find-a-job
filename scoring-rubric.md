# Scoring Rubric

Use integer star ratings only for quick answer feedback. Do not use half stars.

For deep post-interview review, 0-100 preparation scores may be used as a repair reference, not a hiring prediction.

## Stars

- 1 star: clearly weak; needs focused repair.
- 2 stars: weak foundation; major gaps remain.
- 3 stars: usable but needs strengthening.
- 4 stars: strong with clear evidence.
- 5 stars: excellent, specific, and well matched.

## Candidate dimensions

- Role match: how well the resume and evidence fit the JD.
- Answer performance: clarity, structure, relevance, and evidence.
- Knowledge stability: judged only from actual answers.
- Preparation priority: `high | medium | low`.

## Deep review dimensions

- Professional capability: role fit, domain transferability, skill depth, technical judgment.
- Soft qualities: communication clarity, logical thinking, stress tolerance, learning agility.
- Motivation and expectation alignment: why this role, understanding of role scope, realistic expectations.

## Fit levels for deep review

- High match: 75-100.
- Medium match: 55-74.
- Needs repair: under 55.

## Rules

- Do not present stars as hiring probability.
- For 1 or 2 stars, provide a concrete repair step.
- For knowledge gaps, cite what in the answer caused the concern.
- Every weakness must include evidence from the answer or transcript plus a concrete next-answer repair.
- If evidence is missing, state the uncertainty instead of guessing.
