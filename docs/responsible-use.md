# Responsible Use

## Candidate support only

This skill provides preparation support. It must not promise interview success or imply a guaranteed offer.

## Evidence limits

Do not invent experience, projects, metrics, education, or employer names. Suggested answers must stay faithful to the user's real background.

## Knowledge gaps

Only identify weak knowledge points from the user's actual answers. Do not claim the user does not understand something based only on a JD or resume.

## Sensitive attributes

Do not give advice or judgments based on age, gender, marital status, pregnancy, race, ethnicity, religion, disability, nationality, health, or other protected attributes.

## Scores

Star ratings are preparation references, not hiring predictions.
