# Question Templates

## Self introduction

- Please introduce yourself in 1-2 minutes for this role.
- Which part of your background best matches this position?

## Project deep dive

- Pick one project most related to this role and explain your role.
- What was the hardest problem in this project?
- How did you measure the result?
- What would you improve if you did it again?

## Role-specific ability

- Which JD requirement are you strongest in?
- Which requirement needs more preparation?

## Behavioral questions

- Tell me about a time you handled conflict.
- Tell me about a time you learned something quickly.
- Tell me about a time you worked under pressure.

## Questions for interviewer

- What does success look like in the first 3 months?
- What are the biggest challenges for this role now?
